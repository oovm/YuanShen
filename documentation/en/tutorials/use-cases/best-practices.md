# Best Practices

## 1. Start Small

Begin with a simple scenario and familiarize yourself with the system before gradually increasing complexity.

## 2. Make Full Use of Templates

The system provides various company templates and project templates. Select the appropriate template as your starting point based on your needs.

## 3. Continuously Optimize Configuration

Observe the working effectiveness of agents and continuously optimize their configurations and skills.

## 4. Leverage the Secretariat

The Secretariat is the core of a one-person company. Make full use of its coordination and quality control functions.

## 5. Flexibly Use Sub-teams

The sub-team mechanism enables better collaboration between agents. Flexible use can improve efficiency.
