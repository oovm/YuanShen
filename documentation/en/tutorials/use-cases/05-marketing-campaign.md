# Scenario 5: Marketing Campaign Planning and Execution

## Scenario Description

A marketer uses the AI Company system to plan and execute a marketing campaign.

## Organizational Structure

- **One-person Company** template
- **Secretariat**: Responsible for overall coordination and campaign effectiveness control
- **Strategy Team**: Responsible for campaign strategy and creativity
  - Strategy Agent: Develops campaign strategies and proposes creative solutions
  - Competitor Agent: Analyzes competitor campaigns and provides references
- **Content Team**: Responsible for content creation
  - Copywriting Agent: Writes campaign copy
  - Design Agent: Creates campaign materials
- **Distribution Team**: Responsible for channel distribution
  - Distribution Agent: Develops distribution plans and executes distribution
  - Optimization Agent: Monitors distribution effectiveness and optimizes strategies
- **Data Team**: Responsible for data analysis
  - Analysis Agent: Collects data and generates reports
  - Review Agent: Summarizes experiences and proposes improvement suggestions

## Workflow

1. **Strategy Formulation**: The strategy team develops the campaign strategy and creative plan
2. **Content Creation**: The content team creates campaign copy and materials
3. **Distribution Execution**: The distribution team executes channel distribution
4. **Effect Monitoring**: The optimization agent monitors effectiveness in real time and makes timely adjustments
5. **Data Analysis**: The analysis agent collects data and generates reports
6. **Experience Review**: The review agent summarizes experiences and proposes improvement suggestions
