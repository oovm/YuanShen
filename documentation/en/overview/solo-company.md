# Solo Company (SC)

## Why Do We Propose the Concept of "Solo Company"?

After envisioning the "Super Individual," we needed an organizational architecture to realize it. The core motivations for proposing the Solo Company concept include:

1. **Organized Collaboration Framework**: While Super Individuals possess powerful capabilities, they lack structured collaboration mechanisms. Solo Company provides a clear framework for AI agent collaboration by simulating real corporate organizational structures.

2. **Reduced Cognitive Load**: Through familiar corporate organizational structures (CEO, Secretariat, various departments), users can more easily understand and manage AI agent collaboration without needing to learn entirely new abstract concepts.

3. **Specialized Division of Labor Needs**: One person cannot excel in all areas, but through the departmental design of Solo Company, different AI agents can focus on different professional domains, forming complementary capabilities.

4. **Foundation for Scaled Collaboration**: The Solo Company organizational structure provides a clear path for business expansion from simple to complex, allowing gradual addition of new "departments" and "employees" as needed.

5. **Clear Ownership and Control**: Through the CEO role setting, the user's ultimate control over the entire system is clarified, ensuring that data and decision sovereignty remain in the user's hands.

## What is Solo Company?

**Solo Company (SC)** is the organized embodiment of a Super Individual, a complete corporate structure composed of AI agents, but led and decided by a real person (CEO).

### Core of Solo Company

- **CEO (Real User)**: Responsible for strategic decisions, critical judgments, resource allocation
- **Secretariat (AI Team)**: Composed of various AI agents, handling specific execution work
- **Organizational Structure**: Simulates real corporate departments and functional divisions
- **Collaboration Mechanism**: AI agents collaborate efficiently, forming a complete business closed loop

### Advantages of Solo Company

1. **Zero Hiring Costs**
   - No need to interview, train, or manage real employees
   - AI agents are plug-and-play
   - Quickly assemble and adjust teams

2. **24/7 All-Day Operation**
   - AI agents don't need rest
   - Cross-timezone business automatically handled
   - Real-time response to critical tasks

3. **High Scalability**
   - Quickly add agents when needed
   - Seamless scaling as business grows
   - Adjust capability mix on demand

4. **Complete Control**
   - All decisions ultimately made by the CEO
   - Data and intellectual property completely owned by the user
   - Flexible adjustment of strategies and directions

### Customizability

The Solo Company structure can be completely customized according to individual needs:
- **Add New Roles**: Add new AI positions based on business needs
- **Adjust Responsibilities**: Flexibly adjust each agent's scope of work
- **Combine Roles**: Multiple functions can be merged into one agent
- **Simplify Architecture**: Simpler businesses can use more streamlined architectures

## Daily Operation of Solo Company

### Typical Workflow

1. **Task Input**
   - CEO proposes goals or tasks
   - Describe requirements in natural language
   - Set priorities and time requirements

2. **Task Allocation**
   - Secretariat understands task requirements
   - Assigns to appropriate agents
   - Clarifies each agent's responsibilities

3. **Collaborative Execution**
   - Agents collaborate autonomously
   - Automatically coordinate when encountering problems
   - Regular progress reports

4. **Result Summarization**
   - Compile work outcomes from all agents
   - Form complete deliverables
   - Provide suggestions and next-step plans

5. **CEO Decision**
   - Review and evaluate results
   - Make important decisions
   - Provide feedback and adjustments

### Key Advantages

- **Focus on Value Creation**: CEO only needs to make truly important decisions
- **Efficient Execution**: AI agents quickly complete execution work
- **Reduced Errors**: AI handles repetitive work, reducing human errors
- **Continuous Improvement**: The system continuously learns, becoming more efficient over time
